UX UPDATE - 0.2.0-alpha

Primary action: /cta report. Paste this line into a WoW macro, put it on an
action bar, or use Key Bindings > Forever PvP > Report PvP at my location.
There is no confirmation dialog, targeting change, or requirement to open a panel.
The command captures the reporter's CURRENT map/coordinates and zone name.
No adapter attached: explicitly labeled local preview only. No broadcast.

Snapshot: a one-time bounded inspection of accessible target, target-of-target,
mouseover, focus, raid/party and current nameplate unit tokens. No polling.
Includes only identifiable PvP-flagged player characters with a successful,
non-secret UnitDistanceSquared result <=10000. GUIDs deduplicate candidates.
Unsupported distance/identity/flag results are omitted. Unknown-range flagged
identities are counted separately. Snapshot is ALWAYS marked incomplete.
Up to 64 snapshot entries; no claim that all players in 100 yards are visible.
Flagged bystanders are NOT confirmed combatants. A manual observation is a
report (kind='report'), count=0, countUnknown=true; never an automatic heat node.
The backend must establish qualifying fight participants before producing heat.
Reports display in All and Defense. Map report pins open details on click.

Receive UX: a single 300x88 notification anchored top-right, 30px from the right
and 180px down to reduce minimap overlap. Displays title, zone/coordinates, and
known count or 'Fight size unknown'. Left-click opens a 400x270 detail panel;
right-click dismisses. Automatically hides after ten seconds. No audio.
Details contain reporter, reported opposition, note, expiry and Show on map.
Only one notification per 30 seconds; suppressed items remain in activity list.
Mute hides current notification and suppresses future ones, not map/list records.
Duplicate report IDs update records without a new notification.
Backend must assign one stable encounter ID to corroborating users' reports.

Submission safeguards: successful live reports share the persisted 120-second
cooldown with defensive requests. A five-second debounce limits failed retries
and local previews. API/server must enforce cross-user spatial deduplication and
account-level limits; this client alone cannot guarantee either.

Transport v1 extension: accepts kind='report', countUnknown=true and outbound
manual=true, zone=<label>, snapshot={radiusYards=100,complete=false,
unknownRange=<number>,players={guid,name,faction,pvpFlagged,distanceYards,...}}.
Existing incoming alert validation does not retain the raw snapshot; the compact
note summarizes snapshot coverage. The backend stores/aggregates evidence.

Map strategy: Blizzard world map with isolated rendering module. No separate
terrain engine. Backend owns encounters, timestamps, confidence, city state and
deduplication; rendering consumes ordinary mapID/x/y records. Future history,
layers or filters can share these records without replacing the map.

Tests: python tests/test_addon.py and python tests/test_reporting.py
In-game checks still required: slash-command binding, keybinding registration,
secret data handling, toast placement/click in combat, and map rendering.
City raid state and live transport remain backend integration work.
